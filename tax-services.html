Before You Upload

Please ensure you have gathered your files ready for upload based on your filing type:

✓ T4s, T5s, and income slips (Employment, investment, or pension income)
✓ Prior year Notice of Assessment (NOA) (PDF copy from CRA)
✓ Self-Employment: Business expenses, receipts log, and total gross sales / revenue records
✓ Corporation (T2): Financial statements (Income Statement & Balance Sheet) and Articles of Incorporation
✓ Deductions & Credits: RRSP, childcare, and medical receipts to maximize your refund
